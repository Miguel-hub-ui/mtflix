Judge it at your real viewing distance, not inches from the glass. A 55–65″ TV at 2.5–3 m is the harsh test: 0.8 Mbps shows blocking in shadows and banding in gradients, 4 Mbps looks merely soft, 24 Mbps is clean. For reference, a Blu-ray is 1080p at roughly 20–30 Mbps — well below that and you are watching a re-encode, whatever the label claims.
Clips are Creative Commons (Blender Foundation / test-videos.co.uk), so they are safe to stream and cache.
